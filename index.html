"O come, let us sing unto the Lord: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation." — Psalm 95:1

Firestarter Nepal is a Christian movement, formed by Daniel Pariyar, dedicated to reaching hearts through the power of music and the message of the Gospel. Since 2015, we've organized Christian concerts and worship gatherings in Nepal and abroad, touching lives with joyful and spirit-filled music.

After a short pause, we're back with renewed passion and vision to impact this generation for Christ. Music is more than sound to us — it's a spark. We're a community where Christian musicians and young people come together to share their gifts, worship, create, and connect.

With the Gospel at our centre, we provide a platform where fresh voices meet bold expression, championing Christian artists and celebrating Christian music across the world.

Our Vision

To ignite hearts with the Gospel of Christ through music, empowering the next generation to use their God-given talents to glorify Him.

Our Mission

To equip, connect, and uplift Churches, Christian musicians and youth called to serve God through music — encouraging creativity, collaboration, training and gospel-centred impact across Nepal and beyond.
